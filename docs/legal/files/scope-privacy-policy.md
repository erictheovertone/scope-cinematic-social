# SCOPE — PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated: [DATE]**

This Privacy Policy explains how **[ENTITY NAME]** ("Scope," "we") collects, uses, and shares information when you use Scope. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

The short version: **we collect what we need to run Scope, we don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.** Because Scope is built on a public blockchain, some activity is public and permanent by design — that part is explained plainly below.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**1.1 Account information.** When you sign up via our authentication provider (Privy), we receive your email address (or other login identifier) and the wallet address created for your account. You may add profile information: username, display name, bio, profile image.

**1.2 Content you post.** Images, video (including audio), captions, comments, curated collections, and related metadata.

**1.3 Blockchain activity.** Your wallet address and its on-chain activity — minting, collecting, trading — are recorded on the Base blockchain, which is **public**. We read this public data (via infrastructure providers) to display balances, holdings, activity, and earnings in the app.

**1.4 Payments.** Paid memberships are processed by Stripe. Stripe receives your payment details; **we never see or store full card numbers.** We store subscription status and identifiers Stripe provides.

**1.5 Usage and device data.** Standard technical data: IP address, device/browser type, app interactions, and logs necessary for security, debugging, and performance. We use local storage for session and preference data.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To operate the Service: accounts, posting, minting, trading interfaces, wallets display, memberships, notifications, reward programs (e.g., First Cut rewards accounting);
- To maintain safety and integrity: fraud and abuse prevention, enforcing our Terms, complying with law (including DMCA processing);
- To improve the Service: debugging, performance, understanding aggregate usage;
- To communicate with you about the Service.

**We do not sell your personal information. We do not show third-party advertising.**

## 3. The Public-Blockchain Reality

This is the most important section.

- Your **wallet address** and its **entire transaction history** (mints, collects, trades, transfers, reward payouts) are **publicly visible to anyone**, forever, on the Base blockchain — independent of Scope.
- **Minted content is permanent.** Tokens and their metadata cannot be deleted by you or by Scope. Deleting a post or your account removes it from Scope's interface but not from the blockchain (see Terms § 5.1).
- If you share your wallet address, or if someone links your identity to it, they can see your on-chain history. Treat your on-chain activity as public.

## 4. How We Share Information

We share information only with:

- **Service providers (processors)** who run our infrastructure: Privy (authentication and embedded wallets), Supabase (database and media storage), Vercel (hosting), Stripe (payments), Alchemy (blockchain data), and Zora (token protocol APIs). Each receives only what it needs to perform its function.
- **Other users / the public:** your profile, posts, collections, badges, comments, and market activity presented in the app (e.g., "collected by @user") are visible per the Service's design. Your holdings' sizes are not displayed publicly by Scope's interface, but remain derivable from public blockchain data.
- **Legal:** when required by law, subpoena, or to protect Scope, our users, or the public; and in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale (with notice).

## 5. Data Retention

We retain account data while your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete or de-identify personal data within a reasonable period, except: (a) data we must keep for legal, tax, accounting, or dispute purposes; (b) reasonable backup cycles; and (c) **on-chain data, which we cannot delete.** Financial ledgers (e.g., reward accruals and payouts) are retained as records even after account deletion, as permitted by law.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard measures: encrypted transport (TLS), scoped credentials, server-side key management for operational wallets, and access controls. **Your embedded wallet's keys are managed by Privy's infrastructure and are never held by Scope.** No system is perfectly secure; protect access to your login email, and be aware that blockchain transactions cannot be reversed if your login is compromised.

Scope Music

Albums become the organizing unit. Tap any track's artwork and you land on the track with its composer above and the rest of its album below; composer pages lead with albums as square covers, then every track; composers manage titles, covers and their bio themselves after approval; and a user's album covers appear only in the Music section of their bio sheet, never on the profile itself. Mobile and desktop designed together. Waveforms and all names are placeholder data.

4Music on a user's pages

Album covers never appear on the profile grid or header. They live in one place: the Music section of the bio sheet, right-aligned like the Links tiles (mocked in the Augmented Mark set). Users without an album have no Music section.

Build notes

  • Data: an albums table (composer_id, title, cover_url, year, sort) and tracks.album_id + tracks.position. Existing tracks without an album land in a per-composer "Singles" album so every track has a home.
  • Composer page = profile with a composer role: adds bio, links, albums grid, and the manage mode for that user only.
  • Manage edits (title, cover, bio, order) write directly; audio replacement creates a pending version for review. No approval step for metadata.
  • Track view route is shared by the main library, the composer library in the create flow, and the profile music row.
  • Download action is gated by membership tier: shown always, enabled for Augmented Pro, otherwise opens the membership sheet.
